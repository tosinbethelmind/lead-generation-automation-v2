## 2. STRUCTURAL QUANTITY ESTIMATION FORMULAS
1. **Concrete Volume ($V$):** $\text{Length} \times \text{Width} \times \text{Thickness (e.g. } 0.15\text{m for 150mm slab)}$.
2. **Cement Requirement (1:2:4 Mix):** $\text{Bags of Cement} = V (\text{m}^3) \times 7.15 \text{ bags}$.
3. **Sand Requirement:** $\text{Sand (m}^3) = V \times 0.45\text{m}^3$.
4. **Granite Requirement:** $\text{Granite (m}^3) = V \times 0.90\text{m}^3$.
