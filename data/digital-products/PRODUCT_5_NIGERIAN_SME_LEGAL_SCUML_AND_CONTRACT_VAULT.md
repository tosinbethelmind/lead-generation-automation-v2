## 1. THE SCUML CERTIFICATE SELF-APPLICATION BLUEPRINT

To operate a corporate bank account in Nigeria (Guaranty Trust Bank, Access, Zenith, Stanbic) for Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs), you MUST obtain a **SCUML Certificate from the EFCC/NFIU**.

### 📝 Step-by-Step Application Guide:
1. **Visit the Official Portal:** Go to `https://scuml.org/registration/`.
2. **Required Upload Documents (Scan in Clean PDF, $< 2\text{MB}$ each):**
   * Certificate of Incorporation (CAC 1.1 / CAC Status Report).
   * Memorandum & Articles of Association (MEMART).
   * Tax Identification Number (TIN) Certificate from FIRS / JTB.
   * Valid National ID / NIN / International Passport of all Directors.
   * Audited Financial Statement / Statement of Affairs (for companies $> 1$ year old).
   * Evidence of Physical Office Address (Tenancy Agreement or Utility Bill).
3. **Common Reasons for Rejection & How to Avoid Them:**
   * **Name Mismatch:** Ensure the company name on the utility bill matches the CAC registered name.
   * **Incomplete Objects Clause:** Ensure your MEMART explicitly lists your core commercial business activity.
   * **Resolution of Directors:** Attach a signed Board Resolution authorizing the application.

## 2. STANDARD NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA) TEMPLATE

```text
MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

BETWEEN:
BETHEL ENTERPRISES / CLIENT: _________________________________________
AND:
RECIPIENT / CONTRACTOR: _____________________________________________

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION:
"Confidential Information" includes all technical data, customer lists, pricing algorithms, source code, trade secrets, business methodologies, and proprietary systems disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Recipient.

2. NON-DISCLOSURE OBLIGATION:
The Recipient agrees to hold and maintain all Confidential Information in strictest confidence for a period of three (3) years from the date of execution. The Recipient shall not disclose, publish, duplicate, or reverse-engineer any portion of the Confidential Information without prior written consent.

3. REMEDIES & LIQUIDATED DAMAGES:
Any unauthorized disclosure shall entitle the Disclosing Party to seek immediate injunctive relief and liquidated damages of not less than ₦2,500,000 (Two Million Five Hundred Thousand Naira) without prejudice to any other legal remedies.

Signed by Disclosing Party: ___________________ Date: _______________
Signed by Recipient: _________________________ Date: _______________
```
