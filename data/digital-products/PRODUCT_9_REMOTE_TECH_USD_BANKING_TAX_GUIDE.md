## 1. WHY NIGERIAN REMOTE WORKERS NEED A US LLC
Platforms like Stripe, PayPal, and international clients frequently restrict individual Nigerian accounts. By forming a **Wyoming Single-Member LLC**, you gain:
* 100% Legal US Corporate Entity (Zero US Federal Corporate Income Tax on foreign-sourced income).
* Real US Business Bank Account (Mercury Bank, Brex, Relay Financial) with ACH & Wire capabilities.
* Full Stripe US Payment Gateway for processing international credit/debit cards in USD ($).

## 2. STEP-BY-STEP WYOMING LLC FORMATION (TOTAL COST: ~$150)
1. **Registered Agent Selection:** Hire an accredited Wyoming registered agent (e.g. Northwest Registered Agent or Wyoming Agents) for ~$39–$50/yr.
2. **File Articles of Organization:** Submit on the Wyoming Secretary of State portal (`wyobiz.wyo.gov`) — filing fee is $102 (immediate approval).
3. **IRS EIN Application (Form SS-4):** Fax Form SS-4 directly to IRS for non-residents (zero SSN required). State *"Foreign-owned single member LLC for international software consulting"*.
4. **Mercury Bank Onboarding:** Submit Articles of Organization, Operating Agreement, EIN confirmation letter (CP 575), and International Passport.
