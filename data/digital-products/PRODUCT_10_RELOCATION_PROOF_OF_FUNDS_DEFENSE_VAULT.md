## 1. THE 28-DAY & 6-MONTH BANK STATEMENT AUDIT RULES
Visa officers at the UK Home Office (UKVI), Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), and the US Embassy reject applications whenever they detect:
* Sudden, unexplainable large cash deposits right before printing statements ("parking funds").
* Transfers from microfinance banks or fintech apps (Opay, PalmPay) without formal branch stamps.
* Discrepancies between declared monthly salary and inflow patterns.

### 🛡️ The 5 Golden Rules of Clean POF Submission:
1. **Maintain Funds for Minimum 28 Consecutive Days (UK) or 6 Months (Canada):** The balance must NEVER dip below the required statutory living expense threshold for even 1 hour.
2. **Use Commercial Bank Statements with QR Code Verification:** Request statements directly from Tier-1 banks (GTBank, Zenith, Access, Stanbic IBTC) with digital QR verification.
3. **Provide Full Audit Trail for Any Lump Sum:** Every inflow exceeding 2x your normal salary MUST have an accompanying receipt, Deed of Gift, or asset sales contract.
