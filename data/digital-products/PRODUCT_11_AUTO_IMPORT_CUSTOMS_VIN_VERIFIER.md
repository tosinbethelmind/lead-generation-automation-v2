## 1. CUSTOMS DUTY FRAUD IN NIGERIA
Car buyers in Nigeria frequently wake up to find their vehicle impounded by the Nigeria Customs Service (NCS) Federal Operations Unit (FOU) because the car dealer cleared the car with fake papers, hacked assessments, or under-declared duties (e.g. paying ₦500k on a car requiring ₦3.5M duty).

### 🔍 How to Verify Genuine Customs Papers in 3 Minutes:
1. **Locate the Single Goods Declaration (SGD) Document:** Note the **Customs Assessment Number (C-Number)** and Year of Assessment.
2. **Query the Nigeria Customs Service Trade Hub (`trade.gov.ng`):** Verify that the chassis VIN on the paper matches the engine and port release record.
3. **Verify the Bank Duty Payment Receipt (PAAR):** Ensure payment was confirmed through a designated authorized commercial bank with a valid teller/e-receipt.
