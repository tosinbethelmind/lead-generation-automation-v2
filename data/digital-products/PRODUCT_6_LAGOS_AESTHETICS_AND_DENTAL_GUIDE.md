## 2. DOCTOR SAFETY & CREDENTIAL VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

Never undergo dental or aesthetic procedures without verifying these 4 credentials:

1. **MDCN Registration Verification:** Confirm the doctor is registered with the **Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN)** with an active practicing license.
2. **Board Certification in Aesthetic / Maxillofacial Medicine:** Verify international post-graduate training (e.g. British Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry or American Academy of Cosmetic Surgery fellowships).
3. **Sterilization & Autoclave Standards:** The clinic must operate Class-B hospital-grade vacuum autoclaves with single-use sterile barrier pouches opened in front of the patient.
4. **Before/After Unfiltered Case Portfolio:** Request non-Instagram-filtered raw clinical photos of identical cases completed within the past 6 months.
