## 3. 10-POINT "SPOT FAKE LITHIUM BATTERIES" VISUAL PROTOCOL

1. **The Physical Scale Weight Test:** A genuine 48V 100Ah (5.12kWh) LiFePO4 battery weighs **42kg–48kg**. If under 35kg, it contains sandbags or depleted cells.
2. **Smart Bluetooth BMS Voltage Delta:** Scan with the companion app (Pace, Daly, JK BMS). Cell delta voltage must be **$< 0.008\text{V}$ (balanced)**.
3. **Negative Terminal Laser Etching:** Genuine CATL/EVE cells have a permanent laser-etched 24-digit data matrix code, NEVER a paper sticker.
4. **Cycle Life Rating:** True Grade-A LiFePO4 offers **6,000 cycles at 80% DoD**. Reject claims of "10,000 cycles" on cheap packs.
5. **C-Rating Discharge Test:** Must support 1.0C continuous discharge (5kW output on a 5.12kWh pack).
6. **MPPT Solar Input Voltage Window:** Genuine hybrid MPPT supports up to **450V–500V DC input**.
7. **Pure Sine Wave THD:** Total Harmonic Distortion must be $< 3\%$.
8. **DC/AC Surge Protective Devices (SPD):** Verify Class II SPD installation on DC string lines.
9. **Internal Cell Busbar Welding:** Insist on laser-welded copper busbars, not bolted nuts that loosen with vibration.
10. **10% Retention Escrow:** Never pay 100% upfront. Hold 10% for 14 days of faultless commissioning.
