## 📋 WHAT THE DIASPORA CLIENT RECEIVES (SERVICE DELIVERABLES)

When a client books this pass on Selar ($199 / £155 / ₦220,000), our field inspection officers execute the following within **48 to 72 hours**:

1. **4K Ultra-HD Video Walkthrough (Timestamped & Geo-Tagged):**
   * Continuous 15–20 minute unedited camera walkthrough from the access road to the interior boundary walls.
   * Visual inspection of structural columns, lintels, roof trusses, plastering thickness, and plumbing piping.
2. **GPS Beacon & Landmark Verification:**
   * Handheld GPS coordinate reading at all 4 plot corner beacons to confirm the contractor is building on the EXACT plot indicated in the survey plan.
3. **Materials Inventory & Quality Audit:**
   * Physical count of cement bags (checking expiry dates), reinforcement iron rod gauge (12mm vs 16mm specification check), and sand quality.
4. **Contractor / Site Engineer Interrogation:**
   * Recorded audio/video interview with the site engineer on current progress vs. agreed milestone schedules.
5. **Formal Bethelmind Due-Diligence Certificate (PDF):**
   * Executive summary report signed by our certified engineer with clear "Green / Amber / Red" status recommendations before releasing subsequent milestone funds.
